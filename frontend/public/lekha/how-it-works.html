The close cycle

Day 1 to Day 5. Every month.

~120 hrs saved vs manual close
D1

Ingest

We pull your Tally GL, Razorpay / Shopify settlements, bank statements, GST 2B, and TDS data. Normalized into a canonical schema in 4 hours.

D2

Match

Deterministic matching engine processes 80%+ of transactions. Bank rec, GST 2B, TDS classification, vendor matching.

D3

Exceptions

AI agents classify exceptions, draft outreach to vendors and customers, surface judgment calls for human review.

D4

Review

Your accountant on our staff reviews every exception, approves drafts, flags audit risks, prepares variance commentary.

D5

Deliver

Closed books, GST and TDS filings, audit-ready trial balance, audit trail. Delivered to your inbox.

The split

What AI does. What humans do.

AI agents 80%
  • High-volume matching across ledgers, statements, and 2B feeds
  • TDS section classification (194Q, 194C, 194J, 194O)
  • Deduplication and narration tokenization
  • Exception drafting — vendor / customer outreach emails
  • Threshold validation and variance flagging
Human accountants 20%
  • Judgment calls on AI misclassifications
  • Audit defense and Department-of-Revenue queries
  • FP&A commentary on monthly variance
  • Vendor relationship management for complex disputes
  • Sign-off on every irreversible book posting

No agent posts to your books without a human reviewing. No human spends time on what AI can do better. That is the contract.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from Tally or Zoho?

We sit on top of Tally and Zoho, not against them. We are not the system of record — we are the AI-native finance firm that closes your books on top of whatever ERP you use.

How is this different from a CA firm?

We deliver the same outcome at one-fifth the cost. AI does the 80% that a junior accountant did manually. Senior judgment is preserved. Volume scales without proportional headcount.

What if the AI gets a reconciliation wrong?

It does not post to your books. Every irreversible action requires human approval from an accountant on our staff. The AI’s job is to draft and explain, not to commit.

What about data security?

AES-256 encryption, India residency, per-tenant keys, full audit trail. ISO 27001 certification target Q4 2026.

How long does onboarding take?

Tally integration: 24–48 hours. First close: 5 days from data handover.

Do I have to switch off my CA?

No. We work alongside your existing CA firm or replace specific scopes (book closure, GST, TDS). Your call. Many of our customers use us for closure and keep their CA for tax advisory.
