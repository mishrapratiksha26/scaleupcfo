What counts as one “reconciliation”?

One reconciliation = one completed run for a single source. Example: one bank account reconciled against your books for one month = 1 recon; one GST 2B reconciliation against your purchase register for one month = 1 recon; one payment-gateway settlement for one month = 1 recon. The volumes shown on each tier are estimates based on typical usage; we size your plan during onboarding from real data.

Pricing FAQ

Things buyers ask before they sign.

Why so much cheaper than a Top-20 CA firm?

AI agents do the 80% pattern-matching work that a junior accountant used to do manually. CA judgment is preserved — a Chartered Accountant on our staff signs off every monthly close on Scale and Enterprise tiers. Top-20 CA firms (BDO, GT, Nexdigm, Singhi, Mazars, RSM, Nangia, ASA) typically charge ₹50,000–₹2,00,000+/month for equivalent scope. We deliver the same outcome at 50–80% lower cost.

What happens if my volume exceeds the tier limit?

We auto-upgrade you and prorate. You always get reconciled books, never a surprise overage charge.

Is there an annual discount?

Yes — 20% off when paid annually, applied automatically during checkout.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. 30-day notice. We hand off all data in clean format — your books stay yours.

Do I pay before integration is complete?

No. You sign a Purchase Order today. Billing starts only after your first month’s books are successfully closed.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes, in either direction, anytime.
